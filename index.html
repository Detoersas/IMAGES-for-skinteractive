// THE ARCHITECTURE OF FGM

THE DISPLACED PLANE

FGM takes place within a construct known only as the 'Displaced Plane'—a reality where conventional physics are fractured and sightlines deceive. The environment itself is an enemy, shifting angles and collapsing perspective to challenge the player's perception.

We built this world from overlapping planes, mimicking the internal structure of memory and vision. Every location is a study in asymmetrical balance.
